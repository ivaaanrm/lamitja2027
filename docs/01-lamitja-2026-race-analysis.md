## 4. Training style

**Low volume, low frequency, very high quality density.** A hard session was run in **19 of 20 weeks** — on 3–4 runs per week, roughly one in three was a workout. Spanish/Catalan club idiom throughout (*series*, *fartlek*, *rodaje*, *tirada larga*); one session description credits Pol Anfruns.

Fastest km was the **last one (3:27)**. Slowest was km 5 (4:15) on the steepest climb.

HR sat pinned at 170–175 from km 2 through km 18, climbing to 177–182 only in the final 3 km and peaking at 185.

**Course profile (important):** the race climbs for the first 10 km (+140 m) and descends for the second 10 km at a sustained −0.3% to −1.8% grade, with the last 5 km the steepest. The negative split is largely course-driven, but the physiology confirms good execution.

## 6. The single most important finding

The El Tast 10K (25 Oct 2025, 39:42 @ 3:57/km, avg HR 176) predicts a **1:27:17** half by Riegel.

He ran **1:23:33** — **3:44 faster than predicted**.

He raced the half at **identical pace to his 10K** (3:57/km both) at **4 bpm lower average HR** (172 vs 176), for twice the distance.

> **Everything gained between late October and January was pure aerobic endurance, not speed.** The 10K pace did not improve; it was simply held twice as long. And it was achieved on 34 km/week.

## 7. Leverage identified for the next block

1. **Volume is the untapped lever.** 1:23:57 on 34 km/wk with a 46 km peak. Moving to 50–60 km/wk with the same session structure is the highest-return change available.
2. **Frequency before intensity.** 3.6 runs/wk at 33% quality is a high-wire ratio. A 5th easy run is safer volume than a 4th workout.
3. **Interruptions cost real time.** Twelve gaps of 2+ days including a lost week.
4. **The taper was almost nonexistent** — 42.4 km the week before, only the final 4 days easy.
5. **No strength work, no race-pace long runs.** Both cheap additions given the base now established.

## 8. Caveats

- HR figures are from the two races only; the Strava summary feed does not carry HR for the other 69 runs of the block.
- The easy/quality split is inferred from session titles. Interval-session paces include jog recoveries, so aggregate workout paces read slower than the reps actually were.
